- FoodOn, https://foodon.org/ and https://github.com/FoodOntology/foodon . Release 2026-09-20. - FAO AGROVOC, https://www.fao.org/agrovoc/ . English content, snapshot 2026-11-05. English AGROVOC content is available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Only English labels are included here.
- Cultivar descriptions from University of Delaware Cooperative Extension, University of California Sonoma Master Gardeners, the Royal Horticultural Society and Johnny’s Selected Seeds. Individual source URLs and evidence are attached to the records. Selected cultivar names and factual references are used; source pages are not redistributed in full.
